Become a Konkrete Kid

Why Northampton, our home, the path, and how to start.

Why Northampton

Tradition

7 team state titles, 27 state champions, and 43 straight years with a state medalist.

Coaching

A staff that includes a PIAA state champion, several state placewinners, and coaches who have been in the program for more than 20 years.

Training partners

You train every day beside state champions and state medalists.

Competition

The varsity wrestles at the Ironman, King of the Mountain, the Mid-Winter Mayhem, and the Parkland Duals.

Community

Families from kindergarten through alumni, all in the same black and orange.

The path

  1. 1YouthGrades K–6Northampton Black, Orange, and Novice teams in the Valley Elementary Wrestling League.
  2. 2Junior highGrades 7–8The bridge to the varsity room. The 2023–24 team won the District XI title.
  3. 3JVHigh schoolMatches, tournaments, and the JV District Tournament.
  4. 4VarsityHigh schoolDistrict XI, Northeast Regional, and the PIAA Championships in Hershey.
  5. 5CollegeNext levelMembers of the Class of 2026 are headed to wrestle at Elizabethtown and King’s College.

Brayden Wenrich won a District XI junior high title in 2024. Two years later he was a state champion.

Our home

Home matches in Pete Schneider Gymnasium under the state championship banners, and daily practice in the wrestling room.
