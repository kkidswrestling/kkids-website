Parent guide

We all share one goal: helping your child have a positive, successful experience in wrestling — this season and beyond.

Our purpose is to support your wrestler’s growth not only in competition but in life — mentally, physically, emotionally, and morally. The following guidelines are designed to help parents help their athlete succeed.

Keep it fun

  • The #1 reason wrestlers quit: “It’s not fun anymore.”
  • Wrestling should be challenging, but it should also be enjoyable.
  • Encourage effort, improvement, and perseverance — not just wins and losses.
  • The more fun they have, the more they’ll improve.

Focus on growth, not results

  • Winning doesn’t always equal progress.
  • A “good wrestler” is one who keeps improving, mastering skills, and learning discipline.
  • Support their long-term development — not just short-term results.

Be their support system

  • Your role is to encourage, not critique.
  • After tough matches, avoid “teaching moments.” Let your child talk first — and mostly listen.
  • Your home should be an oasis where they can relax, recharge, and feel supported.
  • Say this often: “I love to watch you wrestle.”

Avoid common traps

  • Don’t make excuses or blame officials, coaches, or others.
  • Don’t compare your child to teammates or other wrestlers.
  • Don’t pressure them about weight or performance.
  • Don’t coach from the stands or criticize other wrestlers or coaches — it hurts the team culture and your child’s confidence.

Model sportsmanship

  • “Honor the game.” Be respectful toward referees, opponents, and coaches.
  • Cheer for our team — not against the other side.
  • Your attitude is contagious and sets the tone for your child’s behavior.

Support healthy habits

  • Prioritize clean gear, good sleep, and strong nutrition.
  • Make academics a priority.
  • Help your wrestler arrive early and prepared.
  • Encourage self-reliance — they should learn to manage their responsibilities.

Communication

  • If concerns arise, encourage your child to speak with the coaches first.
  • If the issue remains unresolved, please contact Coach Provini directly — privately and respectfully.

Get involved

  • Join or support the Booster Club, attend matches, and contribute positively to the Northampton Wrestling community.
  • Your encouragement, positivity, and trust in the coaching process make all the difference.
Remember

Wrestling belongs to the athletes — not the adults. It is their thing, not ours.

Together, we can help our wrestlers grow into confident, resilient, and successful young people on and off the mat.

Tournaments

Tournament days are long. Here’s how to plan, follow along, and join us for the postseason.

Weight & health

The coaching staff prioritizes safe, healthy weight management.

Weight certification

Early in the season, every wrestler goes through the PIAA weight certification: a hydration test, then a body-fat measurement. Together they set the lowest weight class your wrestler can wrestle this season and a safe weekly descent plan.

  • Wrestlers must weigh in with a coach every day before practice.
  • You must make your weight before getting on the bus or van for away competitions. You will not be permitted to compete if you are not on weight before departure time.
  • Plastic or rubber sweat suits are illegal and may not be used at any time.
  • Always plan to make weight for a competition unless explicitly told by Coach Provini of a cancellation or postponement.

Skin and injuries

  • Wrestlers must be checked daily for skin infections and report any suspected problems immediately. You must have the NFHS Skin Lesion Form completed by a doctor in order to compete in dual meets or tournaments.
  • Showering immediately after practice and competition is the single most effective way to prevent skin infections.
  • Report all injuries to coaches and the athletic trainer. Our athletic trainers work with the best sports doctors and can get appointments quickly.
  • Injured wrestlers must attend all practices and matches unless noted otherwise by a doctor or athletic trainer.

2026–2027 Northampton Wrestling Honor Code

Purpose

The Northampton Wrestling Honor Code exists to help each wrestler reach his full potential — not only as an athlete, but as a person. Our program emphasizes growth in four key areas: mental toughness, physical effort, emotional maturity, and moral integrity. Success in wrestling depends on all of these working together.

Every member of our team is expected to uphold this code because both the success and the integrity of our program depend on it. By committing to these standards, our wrestlers demonstrate discipline, accountability, and pride in being part of something bigger than themselves. Without question, adopting this code of honor will make our student-athletes better wrestlers, and the coaching staff operates under the assumption that you want our assistance in helping them become better wrestlers.

The coaching staff’s priority is the strength of the program as a whole. We will never sacrifice our values or the team’s reputation for the sake of individual interests. Our goal is to build and maintain a program that earns respect — not only today, but for generations to come.

We want every Northampton wrestler to be proud of his role in this legacy. True excellence requires discipline, self-control, and character. These are the foundations of success on the mat and in life.

With that in mind, each wrestler is expected to uphold the following standards of conduct and commitment.

Team Rules and Expectations

Violation of the following rules may result in suspension or dismissal from the team, as well as the forfeiture of a varsity letter or any team awards. These expectations are in place to protect your safety, your teammates, and the reputation of Northampton Wrestling.

Substance Use

  1. The use, possession, or distribution of drugs, alcohol, or any illegal substance, or being under their influence, is strictly prohibited at all times, year-round.

  2. Athletes may not attend events where drugs or alcohol are being distributed to minors.

  3. Smoking, vaping, or the use of any form of tobacco is prohibited.

Respect and Conduct

  1. Hazing, bullying, harassment, or any form of victimization of teammates or others is prohibited. This includes behavior in locker rooms (home and away), where respectful conduct is especially important.

  2. Defiance, dishonesty, or disrespect toward any member of the coaching staff will not be tolerated. The coaching staff expects each team member to be honest and forthright.

  3. Intentionally exposing others to bodily fluids is strictly prohibited.

  4. Any behavior or attitude deemed to be detrimental to the well-being of Northampton Wrestling is prohibited and can result in dismissal from the team. You are expected to do all that you can to contribute to the success, respectability, and positive image of Northampton Wrestling. This includes being supportive of teammates.

Attendance and Commitment

  1. Attendance at all practices, matches, and team events is mandatory. Unexcused absences are not permitted. Only the following absences are excused:

    1. Missing school because of illness. Text Coach Provini before 2:00 p.m.
    2. A death in the family or a funeral.
    3. A family emergency. The wrestler contacts Coach Provini first. Parents are contacted only if confirmation is needed.
    4. Religious observance, with advance notice.
    5. A required school activity, such as a field trip, a required test, or a teacher meeting with a pass. Come to practice afterward if possible.
    6. A court date or legal appointment.
    7. Anything else approved by Coach Provini ahead of time.

    Appointments scheduled during practice are not excused; emergencies are evaluated case by case. Families have the practice schedule, so please do not schedule appointments during practice except in extreme cases.

Legal and School Policy

  1. Committing any local, state, or federal crime can result in dismissal from the team.

  2. All Northampton Area School District rules apply at all times. Please refer to the NASD Student Handbook for additional guidelines.

Social Media and Digital Conduct

  1. Social media must be used responsibly and respectfully. Inappropriate posts, messages, or images — including those that are offensive, harmful, or reflect poorly on the team — will result in disciplinary action. Represent Northampton Wrestling with integrity online just as you would in person.

Remember

You represent Northampton Wrestling at all times — in school, in the community, and online. Your conduct reflects not only on you, but on your teammates, coaches, and everyone who has worn this uniform before you.

General Rules

Failure to abide by the following basic rules will result in disciplinary action which may include any or all of the following: “positive reminders,” temporary suspension from practice and/or competitions, or dismissal from the team.

Attendance and Punctuality

  1. Above all else, the coaching staff and the entire team must be able to depend on you. You must be reliable and dependable in everything that affects the well-being of Northampton Wrestling. Your punctuality and attendance at school and practice are a must.

  2. If you are ill to the extent that you cannot attend school, you must text Coach Provini at 610-533-3355 before 2:00 p.m. You are also expected to communicate proactively with the coaching staff in the event of any other illness, emergency, or unavoidable conflict.

  3. You are expected to be on time for school every day. If you are unable to practice due to school policy regarding tardiness, your absence will be considered unexcused.

  4. Be a minimum of five (5) minutes early for all practices and matches.

    1. Regular practice starts at 2:55 p.m. sharp and ends at 5:15 p.m. Wrestlers must be in the wrestling room by 2:50 p.m.
    2. Saturday and no-school practices begin at 9:00 a.m. sharp and end at 11:15 a.m. Wrestlers must be in the wrestling room by 8:55 a.m.

  5. Injured wrestlers must attend all practices and matches unless noted otherwise by a doctor or athletic trainer.

Health, Safety, and Hygiene

  1. Wrestlers must be checked daily for skin infections and report any suspected problems immediately. If you have ringworm or any other infection, you might not be allowed to practice. You must also have the NFHS Skin Lesion Form completed by a doctor in order to compete in dual meets or tournaments. Open the printable skin form (PDF)

  2. Wrestlers must shower immediately after practice and competitions.

  3. Be polite and respectful toward our athletic trainers. Follow the rules of the athletic training room. Have treatment completed before practice begins. If you become aware of a medical problem when not in school, call the athletic trainer for advice before making any other decisions.

  4. Headgear must be worn at all times during practice while drilling and wrestling.

  5. The practice area must be kept clean. Make certain to clean up after home match weigh-ins.

Weight Management

  1. Wrestlers must weigh in with a coach every day before practice.

  2. The coaching staff prioritizes safe, healthy weight management. Wrestlers must follow certification rules and descent plans responsibly. If you choose to reduce your weight, it must be done in a safe and sensible manner. Stay hydrated! You are fully responsible for making the weight you agreed to. You must know and understand the weight certification rules and your weight descent plan. If you are unable to abide by the following weight limits, you might not be allowed to compete at that weight class. Suggested fully hydrated weight allowances before practice are posted in the wrestling room:

    1. Two or more days before a match: no more than 4% over.
    2. The day before a match: no more than 2% over.

  3. You must make your weight before getting on the bus or van for away competitions. You will not be permitted to compete if you are not on weight before departure time.

  4. Plastic or rubber sweat suits are illegal and may not be used at any time.

Equipment and Uniforms

  1. You are responsible for any and all equipment and uniforms assigned to you and must pay for lost items that are not returned at the completion of the season.

  2. Do not use or take anything that does not belong to you or has not been signed out to you without permission.

  3. School-issued uniforms, bags, gear, etc. are not to be used for anything other than in-season competition.

  4. During competition, only clothing that has been approved by the coaching staff (t-shirts, sweatshirts, sweatpants, shorts, etc.) is allowed. Everyone must wear the same singlet at the same time for both dual meets and tournaments. This requires planning by team members well before the event.

Academics

  1. You are expected to keep a minimum of a passing average in every class. If you have a grade in a class that falls below passing, you have one week to improve (raise) that grade. If that grade does not improve or it drops after one week, you may receive positive reminders or lose the privilege to compete. This system stays in place until the grade improves to above passing.

Conduct and Behavior

  1. You are expected to exhibit exceptionally good behavior at all times (in school, out of school, in practice, at competitions, every day of the year). Good sportsmanship is expected in all situations at all times.

  2. Swearing, profanity, or inappropriate language will not be tolerated under any circumstances.

  3. Public displays of affection at the site of competitions are prohibited.

Competitions, Travel, and Overnight Trips

  1. The dress requirements for all away competitions are: dress shirt, tie, collared shirt, or sweater and slacks (no jeans or t-shirts).

  2. Cell phones should be used for necessary (i.e., emergency) communication only and not when in uniform or on the bench. The coaching staff may collect cell phones at night during away competitions. If there is an emergency, parents should call Coach Provini (610-533-3355).

  3. You must travel to and from competitions with the team. You also may not leave the competition site without the approval of, and personally speaking with, the head coach first. At individual tournaments and some other special circumstances, you may be allowed to leave the competition with your parents. If permission is granted, you may leave only with your parent(s), who must sign a release form (open the printable travel release form (PDF)). Other arrangements must be made in advance.

  4. During overnight trips, only team personnel (team members on the trip, coaches, and athletic trainers) are permitted in your room. Anyone else must have approval in advance. In addition, team members are only allowed in the rooms of team personnel.

  5. All wrestlers are responsible for making sure the locker room at away competitions is clean before leaving.

  6. When at a tournament, the coaching staff will indicate the time of the team warm-up. You must be dressed, have any taping or athletic treatments completed, have used the restroom, and have any other preparations completed before the warm-up begins. Be prepared to meet with the coaching staff and team approximately 15 minutes before each round begins.

  7. Wrestlers who have been eliminated from competition on the first day of a two-day tournament should be prepared to participate in a workout before competition begins on the second day. Check with the coaching staff for the starting time of that workout.

  8. All team members must be continuously involved in the preparation for home wrestling events until their completion. When coaches indicate all tasks are finished, be prepared to meet briefly in the wrestling room. At the conclusion of all competitions, all team members will meet together with the coaching staff.

  9. Following all school rules on the team bus is required, including but not limited to:

    1. Keep hands and head inside the windows.
    2. Do not throw objects inside the bus or out the windows.
    3. Bags and equipment may not be in the aisle.
    4. No food or drink.
    5. Remain seated while the bus is in motion.
    6. Act appropriately.

  10. When returning home from away competitions, please make arrangements for transportation home from NAHS well in advance of arrival so that your ride is waiting when the bus arrives back at the school.

  11. In the event of inclement weather, cancellations or changes to the competition or practice schedule will be communicated to you by Coach Provini as soon as he is made aware. Always plan to make weight for a competition unless explicitly told by Coach Provini of a cancellation or postponement.

Lineups and Wrestle-offs

  1. In many cases, varsity and JV spots on the team are decided through indicator bouts. However, there are a number of considerations for the coaching staff when determining the starting lineup. Once a varsity or JV position is earned, a challenger must beat the established man two bouts in a row. In the event of a tournament where two varsity starters are competing for the same weight, the best two out of three wins the spot. The coaching staff reserves the right to make lineup changes at any time. Opportunities to “wrestle off” for a starting position can be negatively affected by poor attitude, effort, and/or reliability. Always ask the coaching staff to make sure you understand the situation. This includes (but is not limited to) the lineup and exactly how much you should weigh. Team members are expected to compete for the betterment of the team during the regular season. Wrestle-offs for the postseason may be held for those who wish to change weights and are in good standing (consistently reliable, hard-working, good attitude, etc.).

Remember

Following these rules is not about punishment — it is about preparing you to be the best wrestler and teammate possible while upholding the integrity and reputation of Northampton Wrestling. Excellence on and off the mat requires discipline, respect, and accountability.

Expectations for Parents

The coaching staff will always strive to make parents our partners in accomplishing our mission. We ask the following of every Northampton Wrestling parent and guardian:

  1. Parents should remain in the stands during competitions. Parents should not be sitting with the team during competition. Parents should not be on the competition floor during competition nor in locker rooms at any time.

  2. Please refrain from “coaching” at tournament sites. If there is information you feel must be shared with your child, please contact Coach Provini first.

  3. Athletes are encouraged to advocate on their own behalf. Therefore, in the event of questions or problems, athletes should be the first to communicate with the coaching staff. If a satisfactory resolution is not reached, then parents should feel free to contact Coach Provini.

  4. Coach Provini and his staff will not discuss or justify coaching decisions, including lineups, roles, and other team matters, with parents. It is inappropriate for parents to question or seek explanations for these decisions. Athletes are encouraged to communicate directly with their coaches about their development and improvement.

General Information and Helpful Suggestions

  1. Be aware that attitudes and actions of both athletes and parents will always have an impact on others. Positive attitudes make a positive difference and help everyone. Our program should reflect this in all that we say and do.

  2. Wrestling is a contact sport with the risk of injury always being present. In order to minimize the risk of injury, please observe the following:

    1. Use proper techniques and refrain from using illegal, dangerous, or potentially dangerous techniques.
    2. No horseplay or “ultimate fighting” before, during, or after practice.
    3. A coach must be present during any activity or workout.

  3. It is common for our coaches to demonstrate wrestling technique on and wrestle with members of the team. If you are not comfortable with this, please inform Coach Provini immediately.

  4. In the event of an injury:

    1. Report all injuries to coaches and/or the athletic trainer.
    2. Taping is to be done by the athletic trainer before practice begins.
    3. It is not recommended that you call or see your own doctor without first notifying a coach or the athletic trainer.

  5. Showering immediately after practice and competition is critical to preventing skin infections. This is the single most effective way to prevent skin infections.

  6. Wear clean workout gear for every practice. Shoes and knee pads must be dried by the next training session. Wash knee pads regularly.

  7. Do not transport dirty, sweaty workout clothes and shoes in the same bag you use for clean ones. Use a separate plastic bag for both clean and dirty clothing, shoes, knee pads, etc.

  8. Clothing that advertises alcohol, drugs, or tobacco products, contains vulgarities or profanity, or would otherwise be considered inappropriate or does not project a positive or wholesome image is prohibited. Please wear Northampton Wrestling apparel in practice whenever possible.

  9. Uniforms need to be kept clean. When washing warm-ups and singlets, it is very important that the following washing instructions are followed:

    1. Wash separately in cold water.
    2. Hang to dry.
    3. Do not use the dryer to dry singlets or warm-ups.

  10. Exchange and/or repair equipment after practice.

  11. Keep our practice area and locker room neat and clean. Be sure to take all headgear, knee pads, clothing, etc. with you after practice. Never spit on floors, stairways, walls, etc.

  12. Bring a lock and secure your belongings during practice and matches (home and away). You may keep a lock permanently on a locker in the locker room for the season. Never leave jewelry, money, or any valuables unsecured in the locker room. Remove all items from the locker room at the season’s end.

  13. All required forms must be completed and returned before you may practice.

  14. To be eligible for a varsity letter, the athlete must:

    1. Complete the season.
    2. Compete in a minimum of 50% of varsity competitions.
    3. Score as many points as there are dual meets, or place in a postseason tournament.
    4. Remain in good standing with the team.
