2022–23

Dagen Condomitti finished his career with 118 wins, three District titles, four Northeast Regional titles (just the second four-time regional champion in school history), and three state medals, including 3rd as a senior. Carson Wagner finished with 115 wins, District and Regional titles, and a state runner-up finish, becoming just the fourth four-time state placewinner in Northampton history.
