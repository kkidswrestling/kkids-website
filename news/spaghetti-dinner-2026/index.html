News · October 26, 2026

Spaghetti dinner fundraiser set for November 12

The Northampton Wrestling Booster Club is holding a spaghetti dinner fundraiser on Thursday, November 12, from 5:00 to 7:30 p.m. at The Gin Mill and Grille, 1750 Main Street in Northampton. All proceeds benefit the booster club.

The booster club supports Northampton wrestlers from kindergarten through 12th grade. It covers singlets, warm-ups, travel and hotel costs, and more.

Dinner includes pasta with red sauce, salad and rolls. Eat in or take it home; take-out is available. Tickets are $12 and must be purchased by Monday, November 9, through the ticket form below.

Dine-in guests can also stop by a bake sale stocked with treats donated by Northampton Wrestling families. To donate baked goods, use the sign-up below so we don't end up with duplicates.

Northampton's high school wrestlers will be serving dinner. Thank you to The Gin Mill and Grille for hosting, and to everyone who comes out to support the Konkrete Kids.

Tickets and sign-up

  • Buy tickets ($12, by November 9)
  • Donate baked goods (SignUpGenius)
