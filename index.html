History. Tradition. Community.

Northampton Wrestling was established in 1945. We compete in the Eastern Pennsylvania Conference and are part of PIAA District XI.

Our mission is to provide a supportive and empowering environment where young people develop not only as skilled wrestlers, but as responsible, resilient, and respectful members of our community. We are committed to helping every athlete reach his highest potential on the mat while becoming a well-rounded person who makes a positive contribution to society.

We never sacrifice goodness for greatness. Integrity and honor come first. Winning is the byproduct.
