Reigning state champions

Brayden Wenrich and Gabe Ballard won PIAA titles in Hershey in March 2026. Both are back this season.

PIAA champion at 114 pounds

Brayden Wenrich

  • 36–1 as a sophomore
  • District XI, Northeast Regional, and PIAA champion
  • Fastest pin of the season: 9 seconds
  • 80–7 career record

PIAA champion at 152 pounds

Gabe Ballard

  • 41–5 as a junior
  • District XI, Northeast Regional, and PIAA champion
  • 114 takedowns, 238 team points
  • 121 career wins, a member of the 100-Win Club
