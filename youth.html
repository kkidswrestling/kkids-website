Northampton Youth Wrestling

Our youth program competes in the Valley Elementary Wrestling League (VEWL) for wrestlers in kindergarten through 6th grade. With more than 100 wrestlers each year, the program fields three teams:

  • Northampton BlackVarsity & JV
  • Northampton OrangeVarsity & JV
  • NoviceFirst-time wrestlers

Our goal is to carry on the tradition of Northampton wrestling, teach strong fundamentals, and build a community that lasts long after the season ends.
