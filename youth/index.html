Youth Wrestling

Grades K–6. Where every Konkrete Kid starts.

Northampton youth wrestling

Our youth program competes in the Valley Elementary Wrestling League (VEWL) for wrestlers in kindergarten through 6th grade. More than 100 kids wrestle each year on three teams:

  • Northampton BlackVarsity and JV
  • Northampton OrangeVarsity and JV
  • NoviceGrades K–2

The goal is simple: carry on the tradition of Northampton wrestling, teach strong fundamentals, and build friendships and a community that last long after the season ends. Many of today’s varsity wrestlers and coaches started right here.

Looking ahead

The roadmap

When does a wrestler move?

Wrestlers usually stay put, but coaches can move a wrestler who’s stuck behind a teammate on JV. For example:

  1. 3rd grA wrestler is placed on Orange.
  2. 5th grThey improve a lot, but they’re the same weight as Orange’s varsity starter, so they’re stuck on JV.
  3. MoveCoaches move them to Black to wrestle varsity. Now both wrestlers are varsity starters.

Moves are the exception, made by coaches for the wrestler’s good.

The roadmap

  1. 2026–27: a transition year. Some current wrestlers may move to help balance the rosters. Schedules are set by the previous year’s results and will even out as the teams balance.
  2. Each fall: new Novice grads are placed to balance the teams.
  3. Every season’s end: we review what worked and adjust.
  4. In a few seasons: two equally strong teams.

Questions families ask

Who can wrestle?

Boys and girls in kindergarten through 6th grade.

Does my child need experience?

No. Novice is generally for grades K–2 and focuses on fun and fundamentals. An experienced 2nd grader may move up to Black or Orange.

How are wrestlers placed on Black and Orange?

As a team. Black, Orange, and Novice coaches place wrestlers together, so each team gets an even mix of experience levels and weights. No team is ranked above the other.

Won’t this hold back our more advanced wrestlers?

No. Black and Orange varsity and JV still practice together, so advanced wrestlers keep training with experienced partners.

Will my child still get strong competition?

Yes. Each team has its own full schedule, and Black and Orange wrestle each other during the season.

Will current wrestlers change teams?

Some may, to help balance the rosters. Most of the shift happens gradually, as each new class moves up from Novice.

Isn’t earning your spot a good lesson?

Yes, and wrestlers still earn varsity spots. A JV wrestler can move up to varsity during the season, so there’s always a goal to chase.

What if my child outgrows their team?

We’ll move them. If a wrestler is stuck behind a teammate at the same weight, coaches can move them so both wrestlers get a varsity spot.

Why is this happening gradually?

Big changes work best in steps. Each new class moving up from Novice is placed to keep the teams even, and both rosters balance out over a few seasons. Schedules are set by the previous year’s results, so they’ll even out as the teams do.

How does this help the whole program?

More kids who stay in wrestling means deeper junior high and high school rosters. Wrestlers also reach the junior high as experienced teammates who already know how to compete together.

What happens after 6th grade?

Wrestlers move up to the junior high team in grades 7 and 8, then to the high school program.

Can we still share feedback?

Absolutely. The direction is set, and the details are reviewed every season. We’re always willing to listen: email northamptonareawrestlingclub@gmail.com.
