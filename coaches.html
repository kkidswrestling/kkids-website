Coaching Staff

Head Coach

Joe Provini

In May 2022, Coach Provini was named the 8th head wrestling coach in Northampton history. A 2003 graduate of Nazareth Area High School, he won more than 100 career matches as a four-year varsity starter. He was a District XI champion, placed 6th at the PIAA State Championships, and was named District XI AAA Scholar Athlete as a senior. He went on to wrestle at Shippensburg University, where he was a Division II National Qualifier on a Red Raiders team that set school records with five national qualifiers and an 8th-place NCAA Division II finish, and crowned the program's first national champion.

Coach Provini graduated from East Stroudsburg University in 2012 with a degree in Secondary Education Social Studies. He teaches AP English Language and Composition and AP U.S. Government & Politics at Northampton Area High School.

Before coming to Northampton, he spent 12 seasons as a varsity assistant at his alma mater, helping win three PIAA AAA team state titles (2017, 2018, 2022) and two PIAA AAA State Dual titles (2017, 2020). Over those 12 seasons he coached 10 PIAA AAA state champions, 15 state finalists, and 56 state placewinners.

Through four seasons as Northampton's head coach, he has had at least one state finalist every year and has coached 7 District XI champions, 7 Regional champions, 3 PIAA state champions, and 11 state placewinners.

Varsity Assistant

Mike Sommer

Coach Sommer began his wrestling career in Northampton's youth program. A 1997 Northampton graduate, he capped his Konkrete Kid career with a 4th-place finish at the PIAA AAA State Tournament. At Lycoming College he was a three-time conference placewinner and two-year captain with 97 career wins.

After several years coaching at Whitehall, he returned home as a volunteer assistant in 2012, was promoted to varsity assistant in 2014, and was named District XI AAA Assistant Coach of the Year in 2019. He teaches 8th grade science at Whitehall Middle School.

Varsity Assistant

Jim Derr

Coach Derr has coached in the Northampton program in some capacity since 2001. A 1977 Milton Area graduate, he was a two-time sectional champion, regional runner-up, and PIAA state qualifier.

He became junior high assistant under Keith Venanzi in 2015, when the junior high won two District titles and was ranked #1 in Pennsylvania. As junior high head coach from 2020, he led the team to a District title and a #1 ranking among Pennsylvania boundary schools, and was named the 2022 District XI and PIAA AAA Junior High Coach of the Year.

Varsity Assistant

Marcus Newsom

A Northampton alumnus with deep roots in the black and orange, Coach Newsom was a three-year varsity starter under Coach Don Rohn, Coach Terry Daubert, and Coach Seth Lisa. He wants to help athletes become better wrestlers and to teach life lessons that last well beyond the mat. He has been integral to the development of Northampton's upper weights.

Varsity Assistant

Kyle Rusnock

A 1992 Catasauqua graduate, Coach Rusnock was a four-time state qualifier, three-time state placewinner, and 1992 PIAA state champion. He wrestled Division I at the University of Toledo and is a member of the District XI Wrestling Hall of Fame and the Catasauqua Sports Hall of Fame.

He has coached at Maumee (Ohio), Pen Argyl, Allen, Parkland, and Catasauqua. He is a middle school teacher in the Catasauqua Area School District and Catasauqua's head cross country and track and field coach.

Varsity Assistant

John Paukovits

A Northampton alumnus, Coach Paukovits was a three-time District XI champion (2002, 2004, 2005), a Northeast Regional champion (2004), and a two-time PIAA state placewinner. He joins the varsity staff this season and also serves as vice president of the Northampton Area Wrestling Club.

Varsity Assistant

Ethan Szerencsits

A 2020 Northampton graduate and state qualifier as a senior, Coach Szerencsits wrestled at American University, where he earned a degree in Physics. He trains regularly with the team and has been an integral part of its recent success.
