2021–22

Coach Seth Lisa’s final season, and a junior high team that would change the program.

Dagen Condomitti swept the District XI and Northeast Regional titles at 152, was named Outstanding Wrestler at both tournaments, and placed 4th at states. Carson Wagner was District XI runner-up at 113 and also placed 4th at Hershey, and Christian Fritz reached states at 145.

It was Seth Lisa’s 11th and final season as head coach. He was named District XI Coach of the Year for the second time, and his teams won the 2017 Holiday Classic, the 2017 Cumberland Valley Duals, three Parkland Duals titles, and finished state runner-up in 2019.

The junior high went 17–1 and won the District XI Junior High Tournament, the Cumberland Valley Duals, and the Northern Invitational. Its district champions were Brayden Wenrich, Gabe Ballard, Chase Grabfelder, and Trey Wagner, three of whom would become state champions.
