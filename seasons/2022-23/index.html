2022–23

Coach Provini’s first season, and the end of two great careers.

Dagen Condomitti finished with 118 career wins, three District XI titles, four Northeast Regional titles (just the second four-time regional champion in school history), and three state medals, including 3rd as a senior.

Carson Wagner finished with 115 career wins, District and Regional titles, and a state runner-up finish, becoming just the fourth four-time state placewinner in Northampton history.

Condomitti and freshman Trey Wagner won Holiday Classic titles, Wagner and Condomitti repeated as Top Hat champions, and Condomitti was runner-up at the Beast of the East.
