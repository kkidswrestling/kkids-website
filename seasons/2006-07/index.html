2006–07

Justin Wieller wins the state title at 215 for a young team

Justin Wieller won the PIAA Class AAA title at 215 pounds, beating Manheim Central's Jordan Enck 6–4 in the final. Mike Wechsler (152) placed 5th, Marcus Wilson (171) 6th and Jimmy Sheptock (140) 7th, and Northampton finished 8th as a team.

After graduating five starters, the Kids had six sophomores and a freshman in the lineup early in the season. They won the Cedar Cliff Duals 4–0, beating the host 50–18 in the final, went unbeaten at the NHSCA Festival at Pleasant Valley, and placed 3rd at the Bethlehem Holiday Classic. Easton (36–19 on Jan. 3) and Nazareth (33–21) won league duals, and the Kids tied for 3rd in the Lehigh Valley Conference.

Easton beat Northampton 34–21 in the District XI Duals semifinals. The Kids placed 3rd at the District XI tournament, where Wilson won at 171 with his 100th career win, and 3rd at the Northeast Regional, where Wieller won the 215-pound title.
