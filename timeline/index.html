Timeline

Eighty-plus years of Konkrete Kids wrestling, from the first season in 1945 to today.

1940s1950s1960s1970s1980s1990s2000s2010s2020s

1940s

  1. 1945

    Northampton Wrestling begins

    Harry Wall builds the program and coaches it for 26 seasons, through 1970–71. His teams go 180–110–4 and produce 30 District XI Champions, 11 Northeast Regional Champions, and 2 State Champions.

    Past seasons

  2. 1948

    The first champions

    Albert Billy (95) and Eugene Susko (165) win the program's first District XI and Northeast Regional titles.

    The 1947–48 season

1950s

  1. 1953

    The first District XI team title

    Northampton wins its first District XI team title, at the tournament held in its own gym.

    The 1952–53 season

  2. 1955

    Northampton's first State Champions

    Steve Micio wins the state title at 133 and Edward Keglovits wins the state title at 154. Micio becomes the program's first three-time District XI Champion.

    The 1954–55 season

  3. 1959

    Unbeaten league champions

    Northampton wins the Lehigh Valley League outright at 9–0 and ends Allentown's 41-meet unbeaten streak, 18–15.

    The 1958–59 season

1960s

  1. 1965

    Six straight league titles

    In the middle of six straight seasons with at least a share of the Lehigh Valley League title (1963–64 to 1968–69), Northampton hosts and wins the first league tournament, with seven champions.

    The 1964–65 season

1970s

  1. 1971

    Harry Wall's final season

    In his 26th and final season, Harry Wall's team wins the Lehigh Valley League outright at 7–0. Wall had coached the program since its first season and finished with a 180–110–4 dual-meet record.

    The 1970–71 season

  2. 1971

    Thomas Ortwein takes over

    Thomas Ortwein becomes head coach in 1971–72. In 5 seasons his teams go 28–41–1 and produce 2 District XI Champions and 1 Northeast Regional Champion.

    The 1971–72 season

  3. 1976

    Gordon Bartholomew takes over

    Gordon Bartholomew, a 1958 District XI Champion for Northampton, becomes head coach in 1976–77. In 4 seasons his teams go 32–30.

    The 1976–77 season

1980s

  1. 1980

    Roger Washburn takes over

    Roger Washburn becomes head coach in 1980–81. In 2 seasons his teams go 9–27 and produce 2 District XI Champions and 2 Northeast Regional Champions.

    The 1980–81 season

  2. 1982

    The Don Rohn era begins

    Don Rohn becomes head coach in 1982–83. In 18 seasons his teams go 309–59, win 5 PIAA team titles, and produce 51 District XI Champions, 35 Northeast Regional Champions, and 8 State Champions.

    The 1982–83 season

  3. 1984

    The streak starts

    Sean Finkbeiner wins the state title at 145. Northampton has had at least one State Medalist every year since.

    Sean Finkbeiner's champion page

  4. 1988

    District XI team champions

    Northampton wins the District XI team title.

    The 1987–88 season

1990s

  1. 1991

    Chad Billy, State Champion

    Chad Billy wins the state title at 112, Northampton's first State Champion since 1984.

    Chad Billy's champion page

  2. 1993

    The first team state title

    Northampton wins its first PIAA team title and finishes a perfect 24–0 season ranked No. 1 in the nation. Mike Tomsic wins the state title at 112. The Kids also sweep the District XI Duals, District XI and Northeast Regional team titles. It's the first of three straight state team championships.

    PIAA team champions

    The 1993 title season

  3. 1994

    Back-to-back team titles

    A second straight PIAA team title and a second straight perfect 24–0 season. Whitey Chlebove wins the state title at 130. The Kids sweep the District XI Duals, District XI and Northeast Regional team titles again, and win 50 straight duals from 1992–93 into 1994–95.

    PIAA team champions

    The 1994 title season

  4. 1995

    Three in a row

    A third straight PIAA team title and a third straight Northeast Regional team title. Dennis Liberto wins the state title at 103.

    PIAA team champions

    The 1995 title season

  5. 1998

    Team title number four

    A perfect 21–0 season and a clean sweep: District XI Duals, District XI, Northeast Regional and PIAA team titles. Christian Luciano wins the state title at 171 and John Hard wins the state title at 275. Luciano becomes the program's first four-time State Placewinner.

    PIAA team champions

    The 1998 title season

  6. 1999

    District XI Duals and District Champions

    Northampton wins the District XI Duals and the District XI team title, and shares the Northeast Regional team title.

    The 1998–99 season

2000s

  1. 2000

    Team title number five

    Northampton wins the PIAA team duals title and its fifth PIAA team title, after sweeping the District XI Duals, District XI and Northeast Regional team titles, with a state-record eight place winners at the time. Ryan McCallum wins the state title at 130, and Tommy Rohn sets the program's single-season takedown record with 147.

    PIAA team champions

    The 2000 title season

  2. 2000

    Terry Daubert takes over

    Terry Daubert becomes head coach in 2000–01. In 11 seasons his teams go 199–33, win 2 PIAA team titles, and produce 36 District XI Champions, 27 Northeast Regional Champions, and 10 State Champions.

    The 2000–01 season

  3. 2001

    Jeff Ecklof, State Champion

    Jeff Ecklof wins the state title at 125.

    Jeff Ecklof's champion page

  4. 2003

    Team title number six

    Northampton wins its sixth PIAA team title with four State Champions, still tied for the PIAA Class AAA record: Joey Ecklof (135), Jeff Ecklof (145), Josh Haines (189), and Jon Oplinger (215). The Kids also win the District XI team title. Jeff Ecklof becomes the program's only four-time District XI Champion, its first four-time Northeast Regional Champion, and its second four-time State Placewinner.

    PIAA team champions

    The 2003 title season

  5. 2004

    Team title number seven

    Northampton wins its seventh PIAA team title, and Joey Ecklof (145), Josh Haines (189), and Jon Oplinger (215) repeat as State Champions. The Kids win the District XI team title with a tournament-record 281 points and the Northeast Regional team title, and 10 Kids place at states, still the PIAA Class AAA record. Haines finishes with 157 career wins, still the program record.

    PIAA team champions

    The 2004 title season

  6. 2005

    Northampton's first three-time State Champion

    Joey Ecklof wins the state title at 152, his third straight, becoming Northampton's first three-time State Champion.

    Joey Ecklof's champion page

  7. 2007

    Justin Wieller, State Champion

    Justin Wieller wins the state title at 215.

    Justin Wieller's champion page

  8. 2008

    District and Regional Champions, state duals runner-up

    Northampton sweeps the District XI Duals, District XI and Northeast Regional team titles, then reaches the PIAA Class AAA team duals final and finishes state runner-up. Joe Piro sets the program's single-season records for falls (34) and dual-meet team points (137).

    The 2007–08 season

  9. 2009

    District and Regional Champions again

    A second straight sweep of the District XI Duals, District XI and Northeast Regional team titles.

    The 2008–09 season

2010s

  1. 2011

    Seth Lisa takes over

    Seth Lisa, a three-time State Placewinner for Northampton, becomes head coach in 2011–12. In 11 seasons his teams go 123–57 and produce 16 District XI Champions, 15 Northeast Regional Champions, and 4 State Champions.

    The 2011–12 season

  2. 2016

    Julian Chlebove, State Champion

    Julian Chlebove wins the state title at 113.

    Julian Chlebove's champion page

  3. 2017

    Julian Chlebove, two-time State Champion

    Julian Chlebove wins the state title at 120, his second. Dan Moran becomes the program's third four-time State Placewinner.

    Julian Chlebove's champion page

  4. 2019

    Julian Chlebove, three-time State Champion

    Julian Chlebove wins the state title at 132, becoming Northampton's second three-time State Champion, and sets the program's single-season wins record with 49.

    Julian Chlebove's champion page

  5. 2019

    State duals runner-up

    Northampton finishes true-second at the District XI Duals, then wins its way to the PIAA Class 3A team duals final and finishes state runner-up. The Kids also win the District XI team title.

    The 2018–19 season

2020s

  1. 2021

    Jagger Condomitti, State Champion

    Jagger Condomitti wins the state title at 160.

    Jagger Condomitti's champion page

  2. 2022

    Joe Provini takes over

    Joe Provini becomes the eighth head coach in program history in 2022–23.

    The 2022–23 season

  3. 2023

    Two careers for the record book

    Carson Wagner finishes as state runner-up at 114 and becomes the program's fourth four-time State Placewinner, joining Christian Luciano, Jeff Ecklof, and Dan Moran. Dagen Condomitti joins Jeff Ecklof as the program's only four-time Northeast Regional Champions.

    The 2022–23 season

  4. 2024

    Trey Wagner, State Champion

    Trey Wagner wins the state title at 127.

    Trey Wagner's champion page

  5. 2026

    Two State Champions

    Brayden Wenrich wins the state title at 114, and Gabe Ballard wins the state title at 152.

    The 2025–26 season
